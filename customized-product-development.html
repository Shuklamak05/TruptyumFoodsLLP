Our Customization Process

We collaborate with you at every stage to engineer premium outcomes.

1

Comprehensive Consultation

We start with a detailed consultation to fully understand your product goals, challenges, and desired outcomes. This initial step is crucial for setting the foundation of our collaboration, ensuring that we tailor our services to match your exact specifications.

2

Collaborative Development

At Truptyum Food, we believe in working closely with our clients throughout the entire development process. From selecting the right ingredients to finalizing the product formulation, we maintain open communication to ensure that the end product exceeds your expectations.

3

Prototyping and Iteration

Prototyping is a key phase where your product starts to take shape. We create initial samples and conduct rigorous testing to ensure they meet your specifications. Based on your feedback, we refine and optimize the product until it’s perfect.

4

Customized Freeze-Drying Process

Our advanced freeze-drying technology allows us to precisely control the drying process, tailoring parameters such as temperature, pressure, and time to the specific needs of your product. This customization ensures that the final product retains its quality, efficacy, and desired characteristics.

5

Tailored Packaging Solutions

In addition to product development, we offer customized packaging options that protect the integrity of your freeze-dried product while aligning with your brand’s identity. From single-serve packs to bulk containers, we ensure that your product is market-ready.

Quality Assurance

Quality is non-negotiable at Truptyum Food. Every customized product undergoes rigorous quality control checks, including moisture content analysis, reconstitution tests, and stability studies. We adhere to industry standards and regulatory requirements to ensure that your product is not only high-quality but also compliant with all relevant regulations.
