About TruptYum Foods LLP

Welcome to Truptyum Foods, where we specialize in delivering premium freeze-dried foods that combine convenience, nutrition, and great taste. Established in 2023 and now backed by 3+ years of specialized food preservation and lyophilization expertise, our company was founded on a passion for healthy living and uncompromising quality. Operating from our state-of-the-art 10,000+ sq ft manufacturing facility in Vadodara, Gujarat with an annual processing capacity of 1,400 MT, we serve bulk commercial clients, white-label partners, and consumers worldwide.

We pride ourselves on using only the finest ingredients, carefully sourced from trusted farmers and suppliers who share our commitment to sustainability and quality. Our advanced freeze-drying process locks in the natural flavour and nutrients of our ingredients, ensuring that every bite is as fresh and delicious as the moment it was harvested.

At Truptyum Foods, we’re more than just a food company—we’re a team of passionate individuals dedicated to making healthy, tasty, and convenient meals accessible to everyone. Whether you’re an enterprise scaling private-label product lines, a seasoned adventurer, or a health-conscious family, our facility and team are equipped to deliver at any scale.
